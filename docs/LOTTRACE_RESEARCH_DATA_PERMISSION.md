# LotTrace research-data permission record

Date recorded: 2026-10-21

The project owner states that they have permission from the owners of every supplied archive to use the data for this non-commercial school research project. This permission statement covers the two plate-OCR archives, VMMRdb, UA-DETRAC, and OpenPK material supplied under `datasets/incoming/archives`.

## Permitted project use

- Local research training, tuning, and evaluation for the school project
- Creation of local derived datasets, checkpoints, metrics, and error analyses
- Use of the 31-class Roboflow dataset whose metadata reports `Private`
- Research experiments with the supplied VMMRdb, UA-DETRAC, and OpenPK material

## Boundaries

- Raw archives, extracted images, video, and trained checkpoint files remain excluded from Git.
- This user-attested permission is not represented as a public dataset licence or permission to redistribute third-party data.
- A future commercial release must re-check commercial model/data rights separately.
- Machine-generated annotations are identified as such and are not reported as independent human ground truth.
- Training supervision and final-test ground truth remain separate.

This record corrects the earlier conservative assumption that missing public commercial terms blocked all use. It does not alter the requirement to report provenance and evaluation limitations honestly.
